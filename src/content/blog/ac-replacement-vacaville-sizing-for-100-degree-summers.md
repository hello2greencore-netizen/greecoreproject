**Short answer:** AC replacement in Vacaville comes down to sizing the new system to your home, not to the label on the old unit. That means looking at insulation, windows, which way the house faces, and the condition of your ducts, then picking equipment that can hold a comfortable temperature through a long 100-degree afternoon. Get the size and the ducts right, and the new system cools evenly without running nonstop or spiking your PG&E bill.

## Why Vacaville summers are hard on air conditioners

Vacaville sits in the inland valley, well past the reach of the cool marine air that keeps Marin and southern Sonoma County mild. Summer afternoons regularly top 100 degrees, and the heat often hangs on into the evening.

That means your air conditioner isn't just running on the occasional hot day. From June through September it may run every afternoon for hours. An undersized system can't catch up. An oversized one cycles on and off too fast, leaves the house feeling clammy, and wears out sooner. Either way, the hottest rooms stay hot.

## How we size a new AC for a Vacaville home

The most common mistake in AC replacement is matching the new unit to the old one. If the original system was the wrong size, or the house has changed since it was built, copying that size copies the problem.

Instead, we size the system to the home. On the free in-home estimate we look at:

- **Insulation.** Attic insulation makes a big difference in how much heat pours in through the ceiling on a 100-degree day.
- **Windows and sun exposure.** West-facing rooms and large glass take the hardest hit in the late afternoon.
- **Square footage and layout.** Two-story homes, vaulted ceilings, and bonus rooms over the garage all change the picture.
- **Ducts.** The best AC in the world can't cool a room if the duct feeding it is crushed, leaky, or too small.
- **What's not working now.** Hot upstairs bedrooms, a family room that never cools, or a system that runs all day are all clues.

From there you get clear written options with upfront pricing. Every home is different, so systems are priced to your home after we see it.

## Don't skip the ducts

In many Vacaville homes, the ductwork sits in a hot attic. When ducts leak or have pulled apart, you're paying to cool the attic instead of the bedrooms. Older flex duct can also sag or get crushed over time, cutting airflow to the rooms at the end of the run.

We check the ducts as part of every AC replacement estimate. Sometimes sealing and a few repairs are enough. Sometimes a section needs to be replaced or resized so the new system can actually deliver its air.

## Central AC, heat pump, or mini split?

You have a few good options when the old system wears out:

| Option | What it does | Good fit when |
|---|---|---|
| New central AC (with furnace) | Cools through your existing ducts; furnace handles heat | Your furnace is in good shape and you want to keep gas heat |
| Heat pump | One system that cools in summer and heats in winter | You're replacing both AC and furnace, or want to move away from gas |
| Ductless mini split | Cools and heats individual rooms without ducts | One room never cools, or you have an addition, garage conversion, or ADU |

A [heat pump](/services/heat-pumps) is worth a look for many Vacaville homes. It cools just like a central [air conditioner](/services/air-conditioning), then reverses in winter to heat the house. Modern inverter models adjust their speed to match the load, which helps on those long hot afternoons.

**Repair or replace?** If your AC is 15 years or older and needs a major repair, replacement is usually the better value. Newer equipment uses less power, which adds up when it's running every day all summer.

We'll tell you honestly what we find. If a repair makes sense, we'll fix it. If replacement is the better deal, we'll show you both prices and explain why.

## Rebates, permits, and military discounts

Vacaville is PG&E territory. Depending on current program funding, heat pumps may qualify for rebates through programs like TECH Clean California or BayREN. Funding changes during the year, so we check what's open when we quote your job and handle the paperwork. The federal 25C tax credit ended for systems installed after December 31, 2025.

Permits go through the City of Vacaville. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection. Plenty of Vacaville homeowners serve at Travis or recently did, and military discounts are available. Just mention it when you book. See our [Vacaville service area page](/service-areas/vacaville) for more.

## Common questions

### What size AC do I need for my Vacaville home?

It depends on the house, not just the square footage. Insulation, windows, sun exposure, and duct condition all change the answer, which is why we size every system on site during a free estimate.

### Can I just replace my AC with the same size unit?

You can, but it's a gamble. If the original was sized wrong or the home has changed, a same-size swap repeats the problem. We check first so the new system fits the house.

### How long does an AC replacement take in Vacaville?

Most AC or full system replacements take one to two days. Duct replacement or electrical work can add time, and we give you a clear schedule up front.

### Will a heat pump keep up with Vacaville heat?

Yes, when it's sized correctly and the ducts are in good shape. Modern inverter heat pumps are built for hot inland summers and handle Solano County winters too.
