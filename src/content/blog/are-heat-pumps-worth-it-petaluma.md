**Short answer:** yes. Petaluma's mild winters and warm summer afternoons are close to ideal for a heat pump. One system handles both heating and cooling, runs efficiently in our temperature range, and replaces a gas furnace and a separate air conditioner.

## Why our climate suits heat pumps

Heat pumps move heat instead of making it. In winter they pull heat from outdoor air into your home. In summer they reverse and move heat out.

They're most efficient when outdoor temperatures are moderate, and that describes Petaluma most of the year. Winter nights often dip into the 30s, which modern heat pumps handle easily. Summer afternoons can bring heat spikes when marine air pulls back, which is when you'll appreciate having cooling built in.

## What a heat pump replaces

- **Your gas furnace.** The heat pump becomes your main heat source.
- **Your air conditioner.** The same system cools in summer, so there's no separate AC to maintain.
- **Sometimes your water heater too.** With a system like Harvest Thermal, one heat pump can handle heating, cooling, and hot water.

For rooms without ducts, like additions, ADUs, or converted garages, a ductless mini split is a heat pump designed for one or a few zones.

## What makes the difference: design

A heat pump is only as good as its installation. The most common problems we see, like rooms that never get comfortable or a system that runs nonstop, come from poor sizing or leaky ducts rather than the equipment.

A good install starts with:

1. A load calculation based on your home's size, insulation, windows, and sun exposure
2. A check of your existing ductwork for leaks and airflow
3. An electrical panel review, especially if you're switching from gas
4. Equipment sized to your home, not just matched to the old furnace

That's the design-first approach we take on every Green Core job.

## Costs and rebates

Heat pump systems are priced to your home, since ductwork, electrical, and equipment choices vary. Rebates through TECH Clean California, BayREN, Sonoma Clean Power, or MCE may help, depending on where you live and current program funding. We check what's available and handle the paperwork. The federal 25C tax credit ended for systems installed after December 31, 2025.

## Common questions

### Will a heat pump keep my house warm on cold mornings?

Yes. Modern heat pumps are built for far colder climates than ours. Proper sizing is what keeps you comfortable.

### Is a heat pump loud?

The outdoor unit makes a low hum, often quieter than an older AC condenser. Indoors you'll mainly hear airflow.

### Do I need new ducts?

Not always. We test and inspect what you have. Sometimes sealing and a few changes are enough. Sometimes ductless mini splits are the better route.
