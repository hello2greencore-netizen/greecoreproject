**Short answer:** BayREN (the Bay Area Regional Energy Network) is a partnership of the nine Bay Area counties, including Sonoma and Marin, that runs home energy programs and rebates. For homeowners, BayREN rebates usually go toward energy upgrades like heat pump water heaters and heat pumps, and they run through participating contractors. Amounts and eligibility change with program funding, so the first step is always checking what's open for your home right now.

## What is BayREN?

BayREN is a regional program, not a utility. It's a collaboration of the Bay Area's nine counties, and it offers energy programs for single-family homes, multifamily buildings, and businesses across the region.

That matters in the North Bay because it covers homes no matter who supplies your power. Whether you're in Santa Rosa with Sonoma Clean Power, in San Rafael with MCE, or on PG&E, BayREN is one of the programs worth checking.

For single-family homeowners, BayREN's main offering is its Home+ program. It pairs rebates for specific upgrades with free help from an energy advisor who can walk you through your options.

## What BayREN home energy rebates can cover

BayREN's homeowner rebates focus on upgrades that cut energy use and help homes move off gas. The equipment we see come up most often:

- **[Heat pump water heaters](/services/heat-pump-water-heaters).** These have been a central part of BayREN's home program. They're also one of the easiest gas-to-electric switches for an older North Bay home.
- **[Heat pumps](/services/heat-pumps) for heating and cooling.** Replacing an old furnace and AC with one all-electric system.
- **Related upgrades.** Some projects include work like duct improvements or safety testing that programs may count toward a rebate.

Which measures are offered, and at what amount, changes from year to year. We don't publish a dollar figure here, because a number in a blog post can go stale fast. You can see the current details on the official [BayREN incentives page](https://www.bayren.org/find-incentives).

## How BayREN fits with other North Bay programs

BayREN is rarely the only option. Depending on where you live, these programs may also apply:

| Program | Who it's for |
| --- | --- |
| BayREN | Homeowners across the nine Bay Area counties |
| TECH Clean California | A statewide program for heat pump HVAC and heat pump water heaters |
| Sonoma Clean Power | Customers in its Sonoma and Mendocino County service area |
| MCE | Customers in Marin County and its other member communities |

Some programs can be combined, and some can't. The rules depend on the program and how each one is funded. Funding can also open, fill up, or change partway through the year.

That's why we check at the time of your estimate instead of guessing. It's part of how we work: own the details so you don't have to chase them. For background on the statewide program, see our post on [TECH Clean California heat pump incentives](/blog/tech-clean-california-heat-pump-incentives).

The federal 25C tax credit ended for systems installed after December 31, 2025, so local and state programs like BayREN matter more for 2026 projects.

## Is it worth waiting for a rebate?

Sometimes, but not always. If your water heater is leaking or your furnace has quit, comfort and safety come first. A failed tank water heater can usually be replaced quickly, and a planned upgrade to a heat pump water heater can still happen down the road.

If your equipment is working but aging, you have more time. That's a good moment to call. We can check what BayREN and the other programs are offering, give you a written quote, and let you decide when to move forward.

A few things that help a project go smoothly:

- **Know your power provider.** Sonoma Clean Power, MCE, or PG&E affects which programs apply.
- **Don't buy equipment first.** Many programs need the work done by a participating contractor, with qualifying equipment.
- **Plan for the electrical side.** Some gas-to-electric switches need a new circuit or a panel check. We look at that during the estimate.
