**Short answer:** ductless mini splits are often the best way to add efficient heating and cooling to an older San Rafael home that has no ducts, or has ducts in poor shape. They are heat pumps that deliver air straight into each room, so there is nothing to run through walls, attics, or crawlspaces. For many older homes, that means less mess, a faster install, and comfort in the rooms that never felt right.

## Why older San Rafael homes are a good match for ductless mini splits

San Rafael has a lot of character: older bungalows, hillside homes, and additions built over many decades. Plenty of them were heated with wall furnaces, floor heaters, or baseboard electric, and many never had air conditioning at all.

Adding ducts to a finished house can mean cutting into plaster, finding space in tight attics, or giving up closet room. A [ductless mini split](/services/mini-splits) skips all that. A small outdoor unit connects to one or more slim indoor units with a thin line set, which only needs a small opening in the wall.

San Rafael summers are milder than inland Sonoma County, but warm spells still happen, especially away from the bay. A mini split heats in winter and cools in summer, so one system does both jobs.

## How a ductless mini split works

A mini split is a [heat pump](/services/heat-pumps). In winter it pulls heat from outdoor air and moves it inside. In summer it reverses and moves heat out. Each indoor unit has its own thermostat, so you heat or cool only the rooms you are using.

- **Single-zone:** one outdoor unit and one indoor unit. Great for a bedroom, an addition, or a converted garage.
- **Multi-zone:** one outdoor unit serving several indoor units. Good for a whole home without ducts.
- **Inverter-driven:** the compressor speeds up and slows down instead of switching on and off, which keeps temperatures steady and runs quietly.

## When a mini split is the right call

Mini splits tend to shine in a few common San Rafael situations:

- **No ducts at all.** Older homes with wall or floor heaters can get central-style comfort without building a duct system.
- **Additions, ADUs, and converted spaces.** These rooms are often too far from the main system to heat and cool well.
- **The one room that is always off.** A back bedroom or sunroom that never matches the rest of the house can get its own zone.
- **Ducts that are leaky or undersized.** Sometimes it is smarter to skip the old ducts than to fix them.

## When ducted is a better fit

Ductless is not always the answer. If your home already has ducts in decent shape, a ducted [heat pump](/services/heat-pumps) may be simpler and less visible, since the equipment stays out of sight. In some cases, [duct work](/services/duct-work) repairs or sealing are enough to make a ducted system perform well.

Indoor units do show on the wall, so some homeowners prefer ducted for looks. We walk through both options at the free estimate and tell you honestly which fits your home.

## Common questions

### Do mini splits work for a whole house in San Rafael?

Yes. A multi-zone system can serve several rooms from one outdoor unit. We size it to your home, looking at insulation, windows, sun exposure, and layout, so every zone gets what it needs.

### Are mini splits loud?

Modern inverter mini splits are very quiet indoors, usually a soft hum. Placement of the outdoor unit matters on tight San Rafael lots, and we plan that with you.

### Can a mini split replace my wall furnace or floor heater?

In most cases, yes. Mini splits heat efficiently with electricity and also add cooling, which wall and floor heaters cannot.

### How long does a mini split installation take?

A single-zone system is often finished in about a day. Multi-zone systems take longer depending on the number of indoor units.
