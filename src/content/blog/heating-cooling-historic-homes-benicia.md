**Short answer:** most historic Benicia homes can get comfortable, efficient heating and cooling without tearing into plaster or changing what you love about the house. Homes with no ducts usually do best with ductless mini splits, and homes with an old floor furnace or tired ductwork can often move to a right-sized heat pump or furnace. The key to heating and cooling a historic home in Benicia is planning the route for every line and unit before any work starts.

## What makes Benicia's historic homes tricky to heat and cool

Benicia's older neighborhoods around First Street and the Arsenal have houses that were built long before central heating and air were standard. Many were added onto over the decades, so one house can have three or four different "eras" of construction under one roof.

That shows up in a few common ways:

- **No ductwork, or very little.** Many older homes were heated by a wall heater, a floor furnace, or a fireplace. There's nowhere obvious to run ducts.
- **Plaster walls and original trim.** These are worth protecting, and they're harder to patch cleanly than drywall.
- **Tight crawlspaces and shallow attics.** Some have room for equipment and ducts. Some don't.
- **Drafty windows and little insulation.** Comfort varies a lot from room to room.

Benicia's weather adds its own twist. Breezes off the Carquinez Strait help on many summer afternoons, but heat waves still push in from the valley, and winter nights get cold and damp. An older house can feel stuffy upstairs in July and chilly downstairs in January.

## Ductless mini splits: the gentlest option for many old houses

For a historic home with no ducts, [ductless mini splits](/services/mini-splits) are usually the first thing we look at. A mini split is a small heat pump. One outdoor unit connects to one or more indoor units through a small refrigerant line, about the size of a garden hose bundle.

Why they work so well in older Benicia homes:

- **Small openings.** Each indoor unit needs a hole of a few inches through an exterior wall, not new duct chases through the house.
- **Room-by-room control.** Heat the bedrooms at night and the living room during the day, instead of the whole house at once.
- **Heating and cooling in one.** The same unit cools in summer and heats in winter.
- **Flexible indoor units.** Wall-mounted units are the most common. Some homes can use ceiling cassettes or slim ducted units tucked into a closet or attic.

The craftsmanship is in the layout. We plan where each line runs on the outside of the house, how it's covered, and where the outdoor unit sits so it's out of sight from the street when possible.

## When a ducted system makes more sense

Some Benicia homes already have ducts from a past remodel, or have a good attic or crawlspace to work in. In those homes, a central [heat pump](/services/heat-pumps) can heat and cool the whole house through the existing ducts, often after some sealing and repair.

If you'd rather stay with gas heat, a new high-efficiency [furnace](/services/furnaces) is another path, and it can be paired with central air for summer. We'll walk you through both.

Either way, we size the system to the house itself, not to the label on the old equipment. Insulation, windows, sun exposure, and the condition of the ducts all matter. Older homes are often more forgiving than people think once the worst air leaks are fixed.

| Your home | Option we usually look at first |
|---|---|
| No ducts, wall heater or floor furnace | Ductless mini splits |
| Existing ducts in fair shape | Central heat pump, or furnace plus AC |
| Ducts in poor shape or a tight crawlspace | Duct repair, or mini splits for key rooms |
| Addition or upstairs that never gets comfortable | A mini split just for that area |

## Protecting the house during the work

Respect for the home is the whole job in an old house. Here's how we approach it:

1. **Walk the house first.** At the free in-home estimate, we look at the attic, crawlspace, electrical panel, and every wall we might need to pass through.
2. **Plan every route.** We choose line and unit locations that avoid original trim, built-ins, and the front of the house when possible.
3. **Check permits and review.** We pull permits through the City of Benicia. If your home is in one of the city's historic districts, exterior changes may need extra review, so we check that early.
4. **Protect floors and finishes.** Drop cloths, covered pathways, and a clean worksite at the end of each day.
5. **Walk you through it.** You'll know how to run every unit before we leave.

## Rebates and electrical considerations

Benicia is served by MCE for electricity, with PG&E handling delivery. Depending on current program funding, heat pumps and mini splits may qualify for rebates through programs like MCE, TECH Clean California, or BayREN. We check what's open when we quote your job and handle the paperwork for you. The federal 25C tax credit ended for systems installed after December 31, 2025.

Older homes sometimes have small or outdated electrical panels. Going electric may need a new circuit, and occasionally a panel upgrade. We check this at the estimate so there are no surprises. You can read more about Benicia on our [Benicia service area page](/service-areas/benicia).

## Common questions

### Can you add air conditioning to a historic Benicia home without ducts?

Yes. Ductless mini splits cool and heat through small indoor units and a thin refrigerant line, so there's no need to open walls or ceilings for ductwork.

### Will the outdoor unit be visible from the street?

Usually it doesn't have to be. We look for a side or rear location that's quiet for neighbors, has good airflow, and keeps the front of the house looking the way it should.

### Is a heat pump enough to heat an older, drafty house?

In most cases, yes, when the system is sized to the home. Sealing the worst air leaks and adding attic insulation can make a big difference in how comfortable it feels.

### How much does it cost to heat and cool an older Benicia home?

It depends on the house, the number of rooms, and the system you choose, so every job is priced to your home. We give you clear written options after a free in-home estimate.
