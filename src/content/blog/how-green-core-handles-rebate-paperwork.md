## Why rebate paperwork trips people up

Rebates for heat pumps and heat pump water heaters sound simple. In practice, each program has its own rules, its own funding, and its own order of steps.

Programs such as TECH Clean California, BayREN, Sonoma Clean Power, and MCE may each apply to a different home, depending on where you live, who supplies your power, and what equipment you are installing. Funding can open, fill up, and change during the year.

The most common mistake is doing things in the wrong order. Equipment gets installed first, and then the homeowner finds out the paperwork needed to start earlier. That is why we raise rebates at the estimate, not after the job.

## What we do for you

Here is what "handling the paperwork" means in practice:

1. **We check what is open.** Before we quote, we look at the current program pages for your address and your project, so we are not working from last year's information.
2. **We tell you what we found.** If funding is limited, waitlisted, or closed, we say so. If nothing applies, we say that too.
3. **We keep the price and the rebate separate.** Your quote shows what the project costs and what rebate status looks like, so you always know what you are paying for.
4. **We submit what the program needs from the contractor.** Many programs process incentives through a participating contractor. We prepare and submit that side.
5. **We keep the timeline straight.** We start required steps before install day, so the schedule does not put an incentive at risk.

You can read how one program works in our post on [TECH Clean California heat pump incentives](/blog/tech-clean-california-heat-pump-incentives).

## What we need from you

Some information can only come from the homeowner. Depending on the program, we may ask for:

- Your name and service address
- Your electric provider, so we know which local programs may apply
- A recent utility bill, if a program asks to confirm your account
- A signature or approval on a form, when one is required
- Any income-related documents, only if you choose to apply for an income-qualified option

We only ask for what a program requires. If a program asks for something you are not comfortable sharing, we will talk it through with you.

## What we can't promise

We want to be straightforward about the limits.

- **We can't guarantee a rebate.** Programs decide who gets funding, not contractors.
- **We don't promise amounts in advance.** Rebate amounts and rules change, so we confirm them at your estimate. For current program details, the official [TECH Clean California incentives page](https://techcleanca.com/incentives/) is a good place to start.
- **We can't start a rebate after the fact.** If your equipment is already installed, many programs will not cover it.

The federal 25C tax credit ended for systems installed after December 31, 2025, so we plan around state and local programs instead.

## Common questions

### Do I have to apply for the rebate myself?

Often not. Many programs work through a participating contractor, who submits the project information. We tell you at the estimate whether anything needs to come from you.

### Does Green Core charge extra for handling rebate paperwork?

We do not add a separate line for it. Your quote shows the project price and the rebate status, and your free in-home estimate is where we go over both.

### Which rebate programs might apply to my home?

It depends on your address, your electric provider, and your equipment. TECH Clean California, BayREN, Sonoma Clean Power, and MCE are the ones we look at first. We check what is open for you.

### What if no rebate is available right now?

We will tell you, and talk through your options, including waiting for a program to reopen or going ahead without one. You decide with the full picture.
