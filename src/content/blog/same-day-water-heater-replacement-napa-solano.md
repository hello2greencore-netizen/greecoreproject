## First steps when your water heater quits

Before you call anyone, take a minute to protect your home:

1. **Stop the water if it's leaking.** Close the cold-water valve on top of the tank. If it won't turn, shut off the main to the house.
2. **Shut off the energy source.** Flip the breaker for an electric heater. For gas, turn the control knob to "off."
3. **Contain the mess.** Towels and a wet vac go a long way, especially in a hall closet or interior utility room.
4. **Snap a photo of the label.** Size, fuel type, and model help us bring the right parts.
5. **Call Green Core at (707) 988-5858.** Tell us your town and where the heater sits: garage, closet, attic, or side yard.

If you smell gas, get everyone out and call your gas utility from outside. Don't stay in the house to troubleshoot.

## What can push a replacement past one day

Most failed [gas water heaters](/services/gas-water-heaters) and [electric water heaters](/services/electric-water-heaters) are simple tank-for-tank swaps. A few things can stretch the timeline:

- **Old venting.** Rusted, disconnected, or poorly sloped flue pipe on a gas unit has to be corrected, not reused.
- **Earlier work that isn't to code.** Missing straps, a relief valve line that doesn't go anywhere, or a flex gas line in poor shape all get fixed during the replacement.
- **Switching types.** Going from gas to [tankless](/services/tankless-water-heaters) may need a larger gas line and new venting. Going from gas to a heat pump water heater needs a dedicated electrical circuit and sometimes a panel check.
- **A specific model.** If you want something we don't have on the truck or in the shop, we'll schedule the install for the next available day.

If you need hot water tonight, a like-for-like replacement is usually the fastest route. If you've been thinking about something more efficient, we can talk it through and plan the upgrade properly instead of rushing it.
