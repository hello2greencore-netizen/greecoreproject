## What to do the moment your water heater fails

A few quick steps keep a bad situation from getting worse:

1. **If it's leaking, shut off the water.** Close the cold-water valve on top of the tank. If you can't find it, shut off the main.
2. **Turn off the power or gas.** Switch off the breaker for an electric unit. For gas, turn the gas control valve to "off."
3. **Contain the water.** Towels, a wet vac, or a bucket buy you time. Move anything valuable out of the way.
4. **Call us at (707) 988-5858.** Tell us the type (gas or electric), roughly how big the tank is, and where it sits (garage, closet, attic, crawlspace).
5. **Take a photo of the unit and its label.** It helps us show up with the right parts.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside. Don't wait for a plumber.

## How same-day water heater replacement works

We call it "when our schedule allows" because we'd rather be honest than promise a time we can't keep. When you call, we'll tell you straight how soon we can be there.

For a like-for-like replacement, the day usually goes like this:

- **Quick look and a clear price.** We check the old unit, the location, the venting or electrical, and give you a written price before we start.
- **Drain and remove.** We drain the old tank, disconnect it, and haul it out.
- **Install to code.** California requires a permit and seismic strapping. We also set a properly piped temperature and pressure relief valve, correct venting for gas units, and an expansion tank where one is required.
- **Test and walkthrough.** We fire it up, check for leaks, and show you the controls.
- **Permit and inspection.** We pull the permit and handle the inspection, so you don't have to coordinate it.

## What can keep a replacement from being same-day

Most failed tank water heaters are simple swaps. A few things can stretch the job:

- **Switching types.** Moving from a gas tank to [tankless](/services/tankless-water-heaters) may mean a bigger gas line and new PVC venting. Moving from gas to a [heat pump water heater](/services/heat-pump-water-heaters) needs a dedicated electrical circuit, and sometimes a panel check.
- **Venting problems.** Corroded or poorly sloped vent pipes on an older [gas water heater](/services/gas-water-heaters) need to be corrected, not reused.
- **Tight or awkward spots.** Attics, crawlspaces, and cramped closets take more time and sometimes two techs.
- **Special-order equipment.** If you want a specific model we don't have on the truck or in the shop, the install may be scheduled for the next available day.

If you need hot water tonight, the fastest route is usually a like-for-like replacement. If you're curious about switching to a more efficient type, we can usually get you hot water first and talk through options with you, or schedule the upgrade properly.
