**Short answer:** the SANCO2 is a split heat pump water heater. A small outdoor unit pulls heat from the air and sends it into an insulated storage tank that sits indoors or in the garage. We like it because it is efficient, quiet inside the house, and uses CO2 as its refrigerant. It is a good fit for Sonoma and Marin homes that want all-electric hot water without giving up indoor space.

## How a SANCO2 heat pump water heater works

Most heat pump water heaters are all-in-one tanks. The heat pump sits on top of the tank and pulls warmth from the room around it. The SANCO2 splits that into two pieces.

- **The outdoor unit.** This is the heat pump. It pulls heat from the outside air and uses it to heat water.
- **The indoor tank.** This stores the hot water. It sits in a garage, utility room, or closet, and it does not need to draw heat from the room.

Because the heat pump is outside, the tank indoors is nearly silent. It also does not cool down the garage or closet it lives in.

## Why it uses CO2 as a refrigerant

The SANCO2 uses carbon dioxide, a natural refrigerant, instead of the synthetic refrigerants in most heating and cooling equipment. That is one reason homeowners who care about the environmental side of going electric look at it first.

It is also designed to keep heating water efficiently on cold mornings, which suits our mild North Bay winters well.

## SANCO2 vs. an all-in-one heat pump water heater

We install two kinds of [heat pump water heaters](/services/heat-pump-water-heaters): the SANCO2 split system and the GE GeoSpring all-in-one tank. Neither is better for every home. Here is how we think about the choice.

| | SANCO2 (split) | GE GeoSpring (all-in-one) |
|---|---|---|
| Heat pump location | Outdoor unit | On top of the tank |
| Indoor footprint | Storage tank only | Full unit, needs open space and airflow |
| Noise indoors | Very low | A low hum, like a refrigerator |
| Best for | Tight garages, closets, quiet homes | Garages and basements with room to spare |

If your water heater lives in a small closet or you do not want the hum indoors, the SANCO2 is often the better answer. If you have a roomy garage, the GeoSpring can be simpler.

## Common questions

### Is a SANCO2 water heater loud?

The indoor tank is nearly silent because the heat pump is outside. The outdoor unit makes some noise, similar to other heat pump equipment, so we pick its location with your bedrooms and your neighbors in mind.

### Does it work on cold mornings?

Yes. The SANCO2 is designed to keep heating water efficiently in cold weather, and North Bay winters are mild compared with much of the country.

### Can a SANCO2 replace my gas water heater?

In most homes, yes. It runs on electricity, so there is no gas combustion or flue for the water heater. We check your electrical panel and the space for the tank and outdoor unit first.

### How do I choose between SANCO2 and GE GeoSpring?

It mostly comes down to space and noise. We look at where the unit would go and how you use hot water, then recommend one. You can also compare against a [tankless option](/blog/heat-pump-water-heater-vs-tankless).
