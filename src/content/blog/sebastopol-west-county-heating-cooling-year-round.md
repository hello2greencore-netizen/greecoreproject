**Short answer:** the most comfortable Sebastopol homes pair an efficient heat pump with ducts that actually move air where it's needed, and add a ductless mini split where the house layout makes ducts a poor fit. West county's foggy mornings, warm afternoons, and cool nights are easy on a heat pump, and one system can heat and cool the house all year.

## What makes comfort tricky in Sebastopol and west county

Sebastopol sits close enough to the coast to get marine fog and cool mornings, yet far enough inland that afternoons can turn warm. That daily swing is what trips up many systems.

Add the housing mix and it gets more interesting. West county has older farmhouses and cottages, additions built over the decades, and newer homes built with sustainability in mind. Many sit on larger lots with spread-out floor plans.

Common comfort complaints we hear:

- A damp, chilly feeling in the morning that the thermostat doesn't seem to fix
- A back bedroom or addition that is always a different temperature from the rest of the house
- Ducts running through a crawlspace or attic that lose heat before it reaches the room
- An old furnace or AC that runs constantly and still can't keep up

## Heat pumps for a mild coastal-influenced climate

A [heat pump](/services/heat-pumps) heats and cools with the same equipment. In summer it removes heat from the house like a central air conditioner. In winter it moves heat into the house instead of burning gas.

Our mild winters are where heat pumps shine. They don't have to fight deep freezes, so they run in their most efficient range for much of the season. Many Sebastopol homeowners are also thinking about going all-electric or already have solar, and a heat pump fits that plan well.

Comfort comes from the design, not just the equipment. We size the system to your home, not to the label on the old furnace. We look at insulation, windows, sun exposure, and the condition of the ducts. An oversized system that blasts on and off often feels worse than a right-sized one that runs steadily.

## Ductwork: the part most people overlook

In older west county homes, the ducts are often the real problem. Leaks, crushed runs, poor insulation, and rooms that were never designed into the system all show up as uneven temperatures.

Before replacing equipment, it's worth asking whether the ducts can deliver what a new system produces. Sometimes sealing, insulating, and a few targeted changes make a big difference. Sometimes a section needs to be replaced. Our [duct work](/services/duct-work) page covers what that involves.

## When a ductless mini split is the better fit

Not every Sebastopol home needs a whole-house ducted system. A [ductless mini split](/services/mini-splits) is often the better route for:

| Situation | Why a mini split helps |
|---|---|
| Farmhouse with no ducts | Adds heating and cooling without opening walls |
| Addition, studio, or ADU | Gives that space its own temperature control |
| One room that never matches the rest | Fixes the problem room without redoing the whole system |
| Home with a long, spread-out layout | Zones different parts of the house separately |

Some homes use both: a ducted heat pump for the main living area and a mini split for the space the ducts can't reach well.

## Cleaner air and a more efficient home

Comfort in west county isn't only about temperature. Fog and humidity affect how a house feels in the morning, and surrounding orchards and open land can mean more dust and pollen indoors. A well-sealed duct system, a properly sized system, and good filtration all help.

Efficiency matters too. A system that is designed for your house uses less energy to do the same job, which is the point of working with someone who looks at the whole home instead of just swapping equipment.

## Common questions

### Is a heat pump enough for Sebastopol winters?

For most homes, yes. Our winters are mild compared with much of the country, and a properly sized heat pump handles them well. We'll talk through your house at the free estimate.

### Can I add cooling to an older home with no ducts?

Yes. A ductless mini split can add both heating and cooling without building a duct system, which makes it a common choice for older cottages and farmhouses.

### Why is my house damp and cold in the morning even when the heat is on?

It can be a mix of fog and humidity, poor insulation, and ducts that lose heat before it reaches the room. A home evaluation helps pinpoint which one is the main cause.
