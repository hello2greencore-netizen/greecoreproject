**Short answer:** for most Tiburon homes, the best quiet and efficient option is a properly sized heat pump, either ducted or ductless depending on the house. Tiburon's coastal climate is mild, so you rarely need a big system. What you need is one that is designed for your layout, runs quietly, and keeps every room even.

## Why Tiburon homes need a different approach

Tiburon sits on the bay, and the weather shows it. Winters are cool and damp, and summers are usually mild, with the occasional warm spell. That is gentle on equipment, and it is one reason a heat pump is such a good fit here.

The houses are the challenge. Many Tiburon homes are custom builds on hillsides, with big windows, open floor plans, and rooms stacked on different levels. The sunny, bay-facing side can run warm while the back of the house stays cool.

That is why bigger equipment is not the answer. Oversized systems cycle on and off, make more noise, and still leave some rooms uncomfortable. We size the system to the home, looking at insulation, windows, sun exposure, and duct condition, not to the label on the old unit.

## Quiet options for Tiburon: heat pump choices

Quiet comes from good design as much as from the equipment. Here are the main routes we look at:

| Option | Best for | Why it's quiet |
|---|---|---|
| Ducted [heat pump](/services/heat-pumps) | Homes with ducts in decent shape | Air moves gently through the ducts, with the outdoor unit placed away from bedrooms and neighbors |
| [Ductless mini split](/services/mini-splits) | Additions, view rooms, homes with no ducts | Each room gets its own indoor unit and its own temperature setting |
| Inverter (variable-speed) equipment | Nearly any home | Runs at low speed for long stretches instead of blasting on and off |

Inverter systems are worth a close look in a quiet neighborhood. A variable-speed unit adjusts its output to what the house needs, so it often runs softly in the background.

## Fixing uneven rooms and big windows

If one side of your house is always warmer than the other, the cause is often airflow, not the thermostat. Ducts that are leaky, undersized, or poorly balanced send air to the wrong places.

We start by checking the ducts. Sometimes sealing and a few changes to the runs are enough. Sometimes a section needs replacing. You can read more on our [duct work](/services/duct-work) page.

For a sunny room with a wall of glass, or a room added over a garage, a ductless mini split can solve the problem on its own. It gives that space its own control without touching the rest of the system. Compact indoor units also keep wall space and views clear.

## Placement, access, and installation on a hillside

Tiburon lots can be steep and tight. Before we recommend a system, we walk the property and look at where the outdoor unit can go, how the crew will get equipment to it, and how refrigerant lines and electrical will run.

- **Outdoor unit location.** We look for a spot that is level, has airflow, and keeps sound away from bedrooms, patios, and the property line.
- **Electrical.** Switching from gas may need a new circuit, and some older panels need an upgrade first. We check this at the estimate.
- **Permits.** We pull the permit and coordinate the inspection.

Because every home is different, systems are priced to your home after a free in-home estimate. Depending on your utility and current program funding, rebates may be available through programs like TECH Clean California, BayREN, and MCE. Funding changes during the year, so we check what is open when we quote your job and handle the paperwork. The federal 25C tax credit ended for systems installed after December 31, 2025.

## Common questions

### Is a heat pump quiet enough for a Tiburon neighborhood?

Modern inverter heat pumps are built to run quietly, especially when the outdoor unit is placed well. Where it goes matters as much as which model you choose, so we plan placement at the estimate.

### Do I need ducts to get heating and cooling?

No. Ductless mini splits heat and cool without any ductwork, which suits additions and homes without ducts.

### Can I replace just part of my system?

Sometimes. A mini split can add comfort to one problem room while your existing system stays in place. We will tell you plainly if a partial fix makes sense or if replacing the whole system is the better value.

### Will a heat pump handle Tiburon winters?

Yes. Tiburon's winters are mild, and a correctly sized heat pump handles them well, with no gas furnace needed.
