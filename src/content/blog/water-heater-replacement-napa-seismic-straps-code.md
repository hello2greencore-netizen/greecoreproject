The new unit has to be braced with seismic straps, connected and vented to current code, and permitted through the City of Napa or Napa County. Napa knows earthquakes better than most of the North Bay. After the 2014 South Napa earthquake, a lot of homeowners took a harder look at what was holding their water heater to the wall. Here's what a proper replacement covers, and why the old setup often doesn't make the cut.

## Why seismic straps matter so much in Napa

A full tank water heater is heavy, and it sits on a gas line, two water lines, and a vent. If it tips or walks away from the wall in a quake, any of those connections can break. That can mean flooding, a gas leak, or a fire risk at the worst possible moment.

California requires water heaters to be braced. On a replacement, that usually means:

- **Two straps**, one in the upper part of the tank and one in the lower part
- **Straps anchored into wall studs**, not just drywall
- **The lower strap kept clear of the controls**, so the gas valve or thermostat isn't pinched
- **Flexible connectors** on the water lines, and on the gas line for gas models, so pipes can move without snapping

For a deeper look at straps and expansion tanks, see our guide to [water heater seismic straps and expansion tanks](/blog/water-heater-expansion-tank-seismic-straps-california).

## What "bringing it up to code" means on a Napa replacement

Codes change over time. A water heater installed 10 or 15 years ago may have been fine back then and still not pass inspection today. When we replace a tank, we don't just reconnect the new one the way the old one was hooked up.

Here's what we look at on every water heater replacement in Napa:

| Item | What we check |
|---|---|
| Bracing | Straps at the right heights, anchored to studs |
| Connections | Flexible water connectors, gas shutoff and line on gas models |
| Venting | Correct vent size, slope, and clearances for gas units |
| Relief valve | Temperature and pressure relief valve piped to a safe drain point |
| Pressure | Whether your plumbing is a closed system and needs an expansion tank |
| Location | Clearances in garages, closets, and tight spaces |

None of this shows much once the job is done. But it's the difference between a tank that's just working and one that's installed right. That's the Craftsmanship part of our CORE values.

## Permits: City of Napa or Napa County

A water heater replacement is permitted work in Napa. Which office handles it depends on where you live:

- **Inside city limits** (neighborhoods like Alta Heights, Westwood, or Browns Valley), the permit goes through the City of Napa.
- **In unincorporated areas** like Silverado, it goes through Napa County.

We pull the permit and schedule the inspection either way. The inspector looks at the installation as a whole, including bracing, venting, and the relief valve. A permitted install also gives you a clean record if you sell the house later, since buyers and inspectors often look closely at the water heater.

## Choosing the right replacement for your Napa home

Most calls are a straight swap: gas tank for gas tank, or electric for electric. If your tank has failed, that's usually the fastest way to get hot water back. See our [water heaters](/services/water-heaters) page for the options we install.

If you have a little time to plan, it's worth asking whether a [heat pump water heater](/services/heat-pump-water-heaters) makes sense. A few things to know:

1. **They're often taller and heavier**, so bracing and placement need extra care.
2. **They need space and airflow**, which a garage usually has and a tight closet may not.
3. **They may need a new circuit** if you're coming from gas.
4. **Rebates may be available** depending on program funding. Most Napa homes get electricity through MCE, and we check what you qualify for and handle the paperwork.

If your tank is still working but showing its age, a planned replacement avoids the rush and gives you more choices.

## Common questions

### Do I have to strap my water heater in Napa if I'm not replacing it?

California requires water heaters to be braced, not only new installs. If your existing tank has no straps or a single loose strap, adding proper bracing is often a small job. If the tank is old and showing signs of failure, replacing it may make more sense.

### How long does a water heater replacement take in Napa?

A straightforward tank-for-tank swap usually takes part of a day. Code updates like new venting, a relocated drain line, or a new circuit for a heat pump model can add time, and we'll tell you up front.

### Why can't you just reconnect the new tank the old way?

Because the old setup may not meet current code. The permit inspection checks the whole installation, so we fix outdated venting, connections, or bracing as part of the job rather than leaving you with a failed inspection.

Standard gas or electric tank replacement starts at $2,850. Heat pump and tankless water heaters are priced to your home after a free in-home estimate.
