**Short answer:** for most Windsor homes, the best cooling upgrade is a heat pump. It cools like a central air conditioner in summer, then reverses in winter to heat your home without burning gas. If you only have an old AC and furnace pair, one heat pump can replace both.

## Why Windsor summers are hard on a home

Windsor sits at the north end of the Santa Rosa Plain, away from the coastal fog. Summer afternoons regularly climb into the 90s, and heat waves can run hotter. Winter nights often dip into the 30s.

Many Windsor neighborhoods were built from the 1980s through the 2000s. A lot of those homes still run a builder-grade AC and furnace that are now 20 to 30 years old. The attic ducts that came with them often leak cool air into the hottest part of the house.

That is why upstairs bedrooms bake and the AC never seems to shut off. Sometimes it is not the equipment. It is the system around it.

## Your cooling options in Windsor

There are three common paths. The right one depends on your ducts, your budget, and how your home is laid out.

| Option | Best for | Heats in winter? |
|---|---|---|
| [Heat pump](/services/heat-pumps) (ducted) | Homes with usable ducts that need new cooling and heating | Yes |
| [Ductless mini split](/services/mini-splits) | Additions, garages converted to living space, rooms the ducts don't reach | Yes |
| [Air conditioning](/services/air-conditioning) only | Homes keeping a newer furnace and just replacing a worn-out AC | No |

A heat pump is the "do both jobs" choice. A mini split does the same thing room by room, without ducts. Standard air conditioning still makes sense if your furnace is fairly new and you just need cooling.

## Fix the ducts before you blame the equipment

If rooms are uneven, new equipment alone may not solve it. Leaky or crushed ducts waste the cooling you are paying for, especially in a hot attic.

When we replace a system in Windsor, we look at the ducts as part of the job. We size the system to your home, not to the label on the old unit. Insulation, windows, sun exposure, and duct condition all count. Learn more on our [duct work](/services/duct-work) page.

If wildfire smoke worries you, tell us. Better filtration can be built into the system.

## How a heat pump handles Windsor winters

Our winters are mild compared with much of the country. A properly sized modern heat pump handles North Bay winter nights on its own for most homes. You also get one system to maintain instead of two, and one outdoor unit instead of an AC condenser plus a gas furnace.

Every system is priced to your home after a free in-home estimate. Ducts, your electrical panel, and the equipment you choose all affect the final number.

## Rebates and incentives

Rebates may be available depending on your utility and current program funding, through programs like TECH Clean California, BayREN, and Sonoma Clean Power. Most Windsor homes get their electricity through Sonoma Clean Power. Funding changes through the year, so we check what is open when we quote your job and handle the paperwork. The federal 25C tax credit ended for systems installed after December 31, 2025.

## Common questions

### Will a heat pump keep my Windsor home cool on a 100-degree day?

Yes, when it is sized correctly and the ducts are in good shape. A heat pump cools just like a central air conditioner.

### Should I replace my AC or switch to a heat pump?

If your furnace is old too, a heat pump replaces both in one project. If your furnace is fairly new, replacing only the AC may make sense. We will talk through both at your estimate.

### Do I need a permit in Windsor?

Yes. HVAC replacements need a permit from the Town of Windsor. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection for you.

### Can I cool just one hot room?

Often, yes. A ductless mini split can cool and heat a single room or addition without touching your existing ducts.
