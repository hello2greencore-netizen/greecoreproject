The short answer

With Green Core, a furnace and AC replaced together typically costs $11,000 to $27,300 installed, a ducted heat pump $12,000 to $36,500, and a new gas furnace $5,400 to $13,200. A standard tank water heater typically costs $2,850 to $4,800, and tankless or heat pump water heaters run about $6,000 to $12,600, before rebates, as of October 2026.

What changes the price

Why two homes get different quotes

System sizeMeasured in tons or BTUs. We size it to your home, not the old unit's label.
Efficiency and comfort levelHigher-efficiency, variable-speed equipment costs more and runs quieter and more evenly.
Your ductworkOld or leaky ducts may need work for a new system to perform well.
Electrical, gas and ventingNew circuits, panel work, a bigger gas line or new venting add to the job.

Common questions

Quick answers

How much does a new HVAC system cost in Sonoma and Marin?

With Green Core, a furnace and AC replaced together typically costs $11,000 to $27,300 installed, and a ducted heat pump $12,000 to $36,500, before rebates, as of October 2026.

How much does a new furnace cost?

A standard 80% gas furnace typically costs $5,400 to $8,700 installed, and a high-efficiency 95% furnace $7,400 to $13,200.

How much does a water heater replacement cost?

A standard gas or electric tank typically costs $2,850 to $4,800 installed. Tankless runs about $6,500 to $9,450 and heat pump water heaters about $6,000 to $12,600 before rebates.

How much does a ductless mini split cost?

A single-zone mini split typically costs $4,500 to $14,000 installed. Multi-room systems are priced after a visit.

Why is the price range so wide?

Size, efficiency, your ductwork, electrical and how easy the space is to work in all change the price. Your free written estimate is specific to your home.

Are there rebates?

Rebates may be available through programs like TECH Clean California, BayREN, Sonoma Clean Power and MCE, depending on funding. We check your address and handle the paperwork. The federal 25C tax credit ended for systems installed after December 31, 2025.
